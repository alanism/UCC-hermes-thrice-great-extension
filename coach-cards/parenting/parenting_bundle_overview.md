# Parenting Lens Bundle — UCC Operating Guide

> Five evidence-informed lenses for coaching the adult, not just tutoring the child.
> Date: 2026-07-02

---

## The Five Lenses

| Inspired By | Lens | Core Question | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Emily Oster** | Decision Clarity & Tradeoffs | "What does the evidence say, and what do we value?" | Anxious or overloaded parents comparing options |
| **Alison Gopnik** | Developmental Curiosity | "What if we gave her more room to explore?" | Over-scheduling, micromanaging, treating child as project |
| **Jocko Willink** | Ownership & Discipline | "What do we own, and what standard are we setting?" | Chaos, inconsistency, lack of routine or follow-through |
| **Alan Kazdin** | Behavior Shaping | "What's the positive opposite, and how do we practice it?" | Repeated behaviors that don't respond to lectures or punishment |
| **Becky Kennedy** | Connection & Repair | "Is she good inside? Does she feel seen?" | Emotional escalation, shame, defiance, rupture after conflict |

---

## The Operating Loop

The default sequence when a parenting issue arises:

```
1.  Becky      →  Regulate and connect first
                  (Is the child dysregulated, ashamed, or flooded? Start here.)

2.  Oster      →  Clarify the decision
                  (Is this a tradeoff problem needing data and values alignment?)

3.  Gopnik     →  Check developmental fit
                  (Are we over-controlling or over-structuring what should be exploratory?)

4.  Kazdin     →  Shape the behavior
                  (Is there a repeated behavior that needs a positive opposite and practice?)

5.  Jocko      →  Install routine, ownership, and follow-through
                  (Do we need structure, accountability, and leadership by example?)
```

**Not every situation needs all five.** Step in at the point that matches the parent's current need.

## Runtime Router

| If the parent is... | Start with |
|---|---|
| Panicking, comparing options, asking "what should we do?" | **Oster** — clarify the decision |
| Over-scheduling, micromanaging, anxious about "falling behind" | **Gopnik** — check developmental fit |
| Frustrated by chaos, inconsistency, lack of routine | **Jocko** — install structure |
| Exhausted by a behavior that won't change despite lectures/punishment | **Kazdin** — shape the behavior |
| Facing a dysregulated, ashamed, or defiant child | **Becky** — connect and regulate |
| Unsure where to start | **Becky first** (regulate), **then Oster** (decide) |

## Quick Reference: When to Use Each Lens

| Scenario | Primary Lens | Secondary Lens |
|---|---|---|
| Morning chaos, won't get dressed | Jocko (routine) + Kazdin (positive opposite) | Becky (if shame is present) |
| Homework refusal every night | Kazdin (practice + shaping) | Jocko (routine) |
| Tantrum when told "no" | Becky (validate + boundary) | Kazdin (practice simulations at calm times) |
| Sibling fighting | Becky (attachment threat) + Kazdin (positive opposite) | Jocko (ownership) |
| Child lies about homework | Becky (lie-as-wish vs. lie-as-protection) | Kazdin (practice honesty) |
| Parent anxious about school choice | Oster (Four Fs) | Gopnik (developmental fit) |
| Child says "I'm stupid" | Becky (shame detection, repair) | — |
| Child won't do chores | Jocko (standard + ownership) | Kazdin (shaping + praise) |
| Child has no unstructured time | Gopnik (gardener frame) | Oster (tradeoffs of over-scheduling) |
| Parent snaps and yells | Becky (repair protocol) | Jocko (extreme ownership) |
