# Ghostwriting Integrity Gate

## Overview

Review any AI writing, reading, tutoring, or student-output feature for academic integrity and false mastery risk.

Do not use this skill for ordinary copy editing.

## Procedure

1. Identify what the AI produces
2. Identify what the student must produce
3. Check whether thinking is bypassed
4. Check whether the system records process evidence
5. Add friction that preserves learning
6. Define allowed and forbidden AI help

## Output Format

- **AI role**: what the AI does in this feature
- **Student role**: what the student must do
- **Bypass risk**: how the student could cheat or fake understanding
- **Required student evidence**: evidence the student must produce to prove thinking
- **Allowed help**: acceptable AI assistance (e.g., hints, Socratic questions)
- **Forbidden help**: unacceptable AI assistance (e.g., writing answers, rewriting student work)
- **Fixes**: design changes to close integrity gaps
