### K.OA.5

**UCC capability tags:** `additive_reasoning`, `fluency_automaticity`, `functions_modeling`, `operation_selection`

**Skill examples:**

- Complete the subtraction sentence - numbers up to 5 (K-W.5)

- Make a number using subtraction - numbers up to 5 (K-W.4)

- Subtract - numbers up to 5 (K-W.3)
