# California Common Core Math — Grade 2

> Reference benchmark only. Benchmarks inform. They do not command.

This grade-equivalence view contains 28 benchmark nodes across 4 domains. It is terrain for diagnosis and planning, not a fixed learning path.

## G: Geometry

### 2.G.1

**Cluster:** Reason with shapes and their attributes.

Recognize and draw shapes having specified attributes, such as a given number of angles or a given number of equal faces. Identify triangles, quadrilaterals, pentagons, hexagons, and cubes.

**UCC capability tags:** `counting_cardinality`, `explanation_reasoning`, `geometry_spatial_reasoning`

**Skill examples:**

- Classify polygons: up to 6 sides (2-II.5)
- Compare sides and vertices (2)
- Compare vertices, edges, and faces (2-JJ.5)
- Count sides and vertices (2-II.1)
- Count vertices, edges, and faces (2-JJ.4)
- Cubes (2-JJ.1)
- Draw polygons (2-II.9)
- Identify faces of three-dimensional shapes (2-JJ.6)
- Identify pentagons (2-II.3)
- Identify quadrilaterals (2-II.2)
- Identify shapes traced from solids (2-JJ.7)
- Name polygons: up to 6 sides (2-II.4)
- Sort three-dimensional shapes (2-JJ.8)
- Sort two-dimensional shapes (2-II.8)

### 2.G.2

**Cluster:** Reason with shapes and their attributes.

Partition a rectangle into rows and columns of same-size squares and count to find the total number of them.

**UCC capability tags:** `counting_cardinality`, `explanation_reasoning`, `geometry_spatial_reasoning`, `measurement_units`

**Skill examples:**

- Area (2-KK.3)
- Count the number of squares in a rectangle (2-KK.2)
- Tile a rectangle with squares (2-KK.1)

### 2.G.3

**Cluster:** Reason with shapes and their attributes.

Partition circles and rectangles into two, three, or four equal shares, describe the shares using the words halves, thirds, half of, a third of, etc., and describe the whole as two halves, three thirds, four fourths. Recognize that equal shares of identical wholes need not have the same shape.

**UCC capability tags:** `explanation_reasoning`, `geometry_spatial_reasoning`

**Skill examples:**

- Equal parts (2-LL.1)
- Identify a half, a third, and a fourth (2-LL.11)
- Identify fourths (2-LL.4)
- Identify halves (2-LL.2)
- Identify halves, thirds, and fourths (2-LL.5)
- Identify thirds (2-LL.3)
- Make fourths (2-LL.8)
- Make halves (2-LL.6)
- Make halves, thirds, and fourths (2-LL.9)
- Make halves, thirds, and fourths in different ways (2-LL.10)
- Make thirds (2-LL.7)
