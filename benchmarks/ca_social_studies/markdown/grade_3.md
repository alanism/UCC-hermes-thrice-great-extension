# California Social Studies — Grade 3

> Reference benchmark only. Benchmarks inform. They do not command.

This grade/course view contains 19 benchmark nodes. Content and reasoning evidence may develop nonlinearly across history, civics, geography, economics, sources, and Story Maps.

## Geography of the Local Region

### 3.1.1

**Cluster 3.1:** Students describe the physical and human geography and use maps, tables, graphs, photographs, and charts to organize information about people, places, and environments in a spatial context.

Identify geographical features in their local region (e.g., deserts, mountains, valleys, hills, coastal areas, oceans, lakes).

**Historical reasoning:** `chronology`, `historical_context`, `point_of_view`, `source_analysis`, `spatial_thinking`

**Civic reasoning:** `federalism`

**Geography/economics:** `maps_location`, `scarcity_resources`

**UCC capabilities:** `civic_judgment`, `economic_reasoning`, `geographic_reasoning`, `historical_reasoning`, `map_story_reasoning`, `narrative_reconstruction`, `perspective_taking`, `source_evidence_reasoning`

**Story Map tags:** `people`, `places`

**Skill coverage:** `skills_available`

**Skill examples:**

- Identify Earth's land features using photographs (3-B.2)

- Introduction to Earth's land features (3-B.1)
