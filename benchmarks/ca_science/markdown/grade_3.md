# California Science — Grade 3

> Reference benchmark only. Benchmarks inform. They do not command.

This view contains 18 performance-expectation nodes across 4 discipline areas. Science evidence may be nonlinear across content, practices, crosscutting concepts, and phenomena.

## ESS: Earth and Space Science

### Weather and Climate

**Crosscutting concepts:** Needs manual tagging.

**Phenomena:** `earth_systems`, `energy_waves`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `data_graph_reasoning`

- Use data to describe U.S. climates (3-T.8)
- Use data to describe world climates (3-T.9)

#### 3-ESS2-2

**Skill examples:**

## LS: Life Science

### Inheritance and Variation of Traits: Life Cycles and Traits

#### 3-LS1-1

Develop models to describe that organisms have unique and diverse life cycles but all have in common birth, growth, reproduction, and death.

**Science practices:** `developing_models`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `energy_matter`, `patterns`, `structure_function`, `systems_system_models`

**Phenomena:** `cells_genetics`, `life_science`

**UCC capabilities:** `matter_energy_reasoning`, `model_based_reasoning`, `structure_function_reasoning`, `systems_thinking`

**Skill examples:**

- Compare different animals' life cycles (3-L.4)
- Compare stages of an animal's life cycle (3-L.3)
- Construct animal life cycle diagrams (3-L.2)
- How do flowering plants make new plants? (3-M.5)
- Read and construct flowering plant life cycle diagrams (3-M.4)
- Read animal life cycle diagrams (3-L.1)

#### 3-LS3-1

Analyze and interpret data to provide evidence that plants and animals have traits inherited from parents and that variation of these traits exists in a group of similar organisms.

**Science practices:** `analyzing_data`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `patterns`, `structure_function`, `systems_system_models`

**Phenomena:** `life_science`

**UCC capabilities:** `data_graph_reasoning`, `structure_function_reasoning`, `systems_thinking`

**Skill examples:**

- Identify inherited and acquired traits (3-P.2)
- Match offspring to parents using inherited traits (3-P.1)
- Read a plant pedigree chart (3-P.4)
- Read an animal pedigree chart (3-P.5)

#### 3-LS3-2

Use evidence to support the explanation that traits can be influenced by the environment.

**Science practices:** `argument_from_evidence`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `cause_effect`, `patterns`, `systems_system_models`

**Phenomena:** `human_impacts_engineering`, `life_science`

**UCC capabilities:** `cause_effect_reasoning`, `evidence_reasoning`, `systems_thinking`

**Skill examples:**

- Inherited and acquired traits: use evidence to support a statement (3-P.3)
- What affects traits? Use observations to support a hypothesis (3-N.2)

#### 3-LS4-2

Use evidence to construct an explanation for how the variations in characteristics among individuals of the same species may provide advantages in surviving, finding mates, and reproducing.

**Science practices:** `constructing_explanations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `patterns`, `systems_system_models`

**Phenomena:** `life_science`

**UCC capabilities:** `evidence_reasoning`, `phenomenon_explanation`, `systems_thinking`

## PS: Physical Science

### Forces and Interactions

#### 3-PS2-1

Plan and conduct an investigation to provide evidence of the effects of balanced and unbalanced forces on the motion of an object.

**Science practices:** `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `cause_effect`

**Phenomena:** `forces_motion`

**UCC capabilities:** `cause_effect_reasoning`, `scientific_investigation`

**Skill examples:**

- How do balanced and unbalanced forces affect motion? (3-G.2)
- How do mass and force affect motion? (3-G.3)
- Identify pushes and pulls (3-G.1)

#### 3-PS2-2

Make observations and/or measurements of an object's motion to provide evidence that a pattern can be used to predict future motion.

**Science practices:** `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `patterns`

**Phenomena:** `forces_motion`

**UCC capabilities:** `scientific_investigation`

#### 3-PS2-3

Ask questions to determine cause and effect relationships of electric or magnetic interactions between two objects not in contact with each other.

**Science practices:** `asking_questions`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `cause_effect`

**Phenomena:** `forces_motion`

**UCC capabilities:** `cause_effect_reasoning`

**Skill examples:**

- Compare strengths of magnetic forces (3-J.3)
- Identify magnets that attract or repel (3-J.1)
- Introduction to static electricity and charged objects (3-I.1)
- Label magnets that attract or repel (3-J.2)

**Science practices:** `asking_questions`, `designing_solutions`

**Crosscutting concepts:** Needs manual tagging.

**Phenomena:** `forces_motion`

**UCC capabilities:** `engineering_design`

**Skill examples:**

- Solve problems using magnets (3-X.1)
