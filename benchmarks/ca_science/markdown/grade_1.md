# California Science — Grade 1

> Reference benchmark only. Benchmarks inform. They do not command.

This view contains 12 performance-expectation nodes across 4 discipline areas. Science evidence may be nonlinear across content, practices, crosscutting concepts, and phenomena.

## ESS: Earth and Space Science

### Earth's Place in the Universe

#### 1-ESS1-1

Use observations of the sun, moon, and stars to describe patterns that can be predicted.

**Science practices:** `analyzing_data`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `patterns`

**Phenomena:** `earth_systems`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `data_graph_reasoning`

**Skill examples:**

- The daytime sky (1-O.1)
- The night sky (1-O.2)

#### 1-ESS1-2

Make observations at different times of year to relate the amount of daylight to the time of year.

**Science practices:** `analyzing_data`, `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `patterns`, `scale_proportion_quantity`

**Phenomena:** `earth_systems`, `energy_waves`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `data_graph_reasoning`, `scientific_investigation`

**Skill examples:**

- Seasonal daylight patterns (1-O.)

## ETS: Engineering Design

### Engineering Design

#### K-2-ETS1-1

Ask questions, make observations, and gather information about a situation people want to change to define a simple problem that can be solved through the development of a new or improved object or tool.

**Science practices:** `asking_questions`, `designing_solutions`, `obtaining_information`, `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `stability_change`

**Phenomena:** `human_impacts_engineering`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `engineering_design`, `scientific_communication`, `scientific_investigation`

**Skill examples:**

- Design a race car track (1-P.1)
- Design a sunshade (1-P.2)
- Design knee pads (1-P.)

#### K-2-ETS1-2

Develop a simple sketch, drawing, or physical model to illustrate how the shape of an object helps it function as needed to solve a given problem.

**Science practices:** `designing_solutions`, `developing_models`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `structure_function`

**Phenomena:** `human_impacts_engineering`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `engineering_design`, `model_based_reasoning`, `structure_function_reasoning`

**Skill examples:**

- Design a race car track (1-P.1)
- Design a sunshade (1-P.2)

#### K-2-ETS1-3

Analyze data from tests of two objects designed to solve the same problem to compare the strengths and weaknesses of how each performs.

**Science practices:** `analyzing_data`, `designing_solutions`

**Crosscutting concepts:** Needs manual tagging.

**Phenomena:** `human_impacts_engineering`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `data_graph_reasoning`, `engineering_design`

**Skill examples:**

- Design a race car track (1-P.1)
- Design a sunshade (1-P.2)
- Design knee pads (1-P.)

## LS: Life Science

### From Molecules to Organisms: Structures and Processes

#### 1-LS1-1

Use materials to design a solution to a human problem by mimicking how plants and/or animals use their external parts to help them survive, grow, and meet their needs.

**Science practices:** `designing_solutions`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `structure_function`

**Phenomena:** `life_science`, `matter_chemistry`

**UCC capabilities:** `engineering_design`, `structure_function_reasoning`

**Skill examples:**

- Design knee pads (1-P.)
- How do animals use their body parts? (1-H.2)
- Identify plant parts (1-I.2)
- Identify what plant parts do (1-I.3)

#### 1-LS1-2

Read texts and use media to determine patterns in behavior of parents and offspring that help offspring survive.

**Science practices:** `obtaining_information`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `patterns`, `structure_function`

**Phenomena:** `life_science`, `matter_chemistry`

**UCC capabilities:** `scientific_communication`, `structure_function_reasoning`

### Heredity: Inheritance and Variation of Traits

#### 1-LS3-1

Make observations to construct an evidence-based account that young plants and animals are like, but not exactly like, their parents.

**Science practices:** `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** Needs manual tagging.

**Phenomena:** `life_science`

**UCC capabilities:** `scientific_investigation`

**Skill examples:**

- Compare young living things to their parents (1-J.4)
- Match animals to their parents (1-H.3)
- Match plants to their parents (1-I.4)

## PS: Physical Science

### Waves and Their Applications in Technologies for Information Transfer

#### 1-PS4-1

Plan and conduct investigations to provide evidence that vibrating materials can make sound and that sound can make materials vibrate.

**Science practices:** `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `energy_matter`

**Phenomena:** `energy_waves`, `matter_chemistry`

**UCC capabilities:** `matter_energy_reasoning`, `scientific_investigation`

#### 1-PS4-2

Make observations to construct an evidence-based account that objects in darkness can be seen only when illuminated.

**Science practices:** `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `energy_matter`

**Phenomena:** `energy_waves`

**UCC capabilities:** `matter_energy_reasoning`, `scientific_investigation`

**Skill examples:**

- More light or less light (1-F.1)

#### 1-PS4-3

Plan and conduct an investigation to determine the effect of placing objects made with different materials in the path of a beam of light.

**Science practices:** `planning_investigations`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `cause_effect`, `energy_matter`

**Phenomena:** `energy_waves`, `matter_chemistry`

**UCC capabilities:** `cause_effect_reasoning`, `matter_energy_reasoning`, `scientific_investigation`

**Skill examples:**

- Investigate shadows (1-F.2)
- Which lets light go through? (1-F.3)

#### 1-PS4-4

Use tools and materials to design and build a device that uses light or sound to solve the problem of communicating over a distance.

**Science practices:** `designing_solutions`, `obtaining_information`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `energy_matter`, `scale_proportion_quantity`

**Phenomena:** `energy_waves`, `matter_chemistry`

**UCC capabilities:** `engineering_design`, `matter_energy_reasoning`, `scientific_communication`

**Skill examples:**

- How do we use light and sound to communicate? (1-F.4)
