# California Science — Grade 8

> Reference benchmark only. Benchmarks inform. They do not command.

This view contains 26 performance-expectation nodes across 4 discipline areas. Science evidence may be nonlinear across content, practices, crosscutting concepts, and phenomena.

## ESS: Earth and Space Science

### Space Systems

#### MS-ESS1-1

Develop and use a model of the Earth-sun-moon system to describe the cyclic patterns of lunar phases, eclipses of the sun and moon, and seasons.

**Science practices:** `developing_models`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `cause_effect`, `patterns`, `systems_system_models`

**Phenomena:** `earth_systems`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `cause_effect_reasoning`, `model_based_reasoning`, `systems_thinking`

**Skill examples:**

- Analyze models of the Earth-Sun-Moon system (8-HH.1)
- Identify phases of the Moon (8-HH.2)
- Lunar eclipses (8-HH.4)
- Solar eclipses (8-HH.3)
- What causes the seasons on Earth? (8-HH.5)
