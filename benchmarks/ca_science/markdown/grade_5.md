# California Science — Grade 5

> Reference benchmark only. Benchmarks inform. They do not command.

This view contains 16 performance-expectation nodes across 4 discipline areas. Science evidence may be nonlinear across content, practices, crosscutting concepts, and phenomena.

## ESS: Earth and Space Science

### Space Systems: Stars and the Solar System

#### 5-ESS1-1

Support an argument that differences in the apparent brightness of the sun compared to other stars is due to their relative distances from Earth.

**Science practices:** `argument_from_evidence`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `scale_proportion_quantity`, `systems_system_models`

**Phenomena:** `earth_systems`, `life_science`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `evidence_reasoning`, `systems_thinking`

**Skill examples:**

- Brightness of the Sun and other stars (5-Y.4)

#### 5-ESS1-2

Represent data in graphical displays to reveal patterns of daily changes in length and direction of shadows, day and night, and the seasonal appearance of some stars in the night sky.

**Science practices:** `analyzing_data`

**Crosscutting concepts:** `patterns`, `stability_change`, `systems_system_models`

**Phenomena:** `earth_systems`, `space_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `data_graph_reasoning`, `systems_thinking`

**Skill examples:**

- Constellations and the changing night sky (5-Y.5)
- Day and night (5-Y.3)
- Earth's rotation and orbit (5-Y.2)
- Shadows (5-Y.1)

**Phenomena:** `earth_systems`

**UCC capabilities:** `model_based_reasoning`, `structure_function_reasoning`, `systems_thinking`

- Select parts of water cycle diagrams (5-W.3)

- Describe and graph water on Earth (5-W.1)

**UCC capabilities:** `engineering_design`

**Skill examples:**

**Science practices:** `designing_solutions`

**Crosscutting concepts:** Needs manual tagging.

**Phenomena:** `cells_genetics`, `human_impacts_engineering`

**UCC capabilities:** `engineering_design`

**Skill examples:**
