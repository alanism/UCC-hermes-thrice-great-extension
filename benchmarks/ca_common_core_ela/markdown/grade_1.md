# California Common Core ELA — Grade 1

> Reference benchmark only. Benchmarks inform. They do not command.

This grade-equivalence view contains 19 PI/PII benchmark nodes. ELA capabilities spiral across grades; this page is reference terrain rather than a fixed learning path.

## PI.1: Interacting in Meaningful Ways

### PI.1.A: Collaborative

#### PI.1.1

Exchanging information and ideas with others through oral collaborative conversations on a range of social and academic topics

**UCC capability tags:** `collaborative_discussion`

**Skill examples:**

- Who, what, when, where, or why? (1-RR.7)

#### PI.1.2

Interacting with others in written English in various communicative forms (print, communicative technology, and multimedia)

**UCC capability tags:** `collaborative_discussion`, `reading_comprehension`, `rhetoric_media_literacy`

#### PI.1.3

Offering and supporting opinions and negotiating with others in communicative exchanges

**UCC capability tags:** `argument_claim_evidence`, `language_conventions`, `reading_comprehension`

**Skill examples:**

- Complete the fact and opinion sentences (1)
- Complete the opinion passage with a reason (1)
- Complete the opinion passage with an example (1)

#### PI.1.4

Adapting language choices to various contexts (based on task, purpose, audience, and text type)
